About Al Wasit

Building the Middle East.
One Machine at a Time.

In 1985, Al Wasit began with a handful of machines and a simple promise: show up, do the job right, and keep the fleet running. Four decades on, that promise hasn't changed, only the scale has.

Today we're a one-stop source for earthmoving and road equipment, materials handling, work tools, attachments and spare parts, trusted by contractors in more than 30 countries across the Middle East, Africa, Asia and the CIS. Our own Plant Hire & Transportation division keeps projects moving, from a single excavator on a quarry job to a full fleet on a federal infrastructure programme.
