Frequently Asked Questions

Buying, renting, spare parts and after-sales support, answered.

What does Al Wasit Machinery do?

Since 1985, Al Wasit has supplied heavy construction equipment across the Middle East, Africa, Asia and the CIS. We sell new and used machinery, run a plant-hire & transportation division, and supply genuine and OEM spare parts backed by full after-sales service.

Do you sell both new and used equipment?

Yes. We offer our own AWM / Awashi new range plus CAMC commercial vehicles, alongside an inspected fleet of used and rental machines from leading global brands.

Can I rent equipment instead of buying?

Yes, our Plant Hire division offers flexible short and long-term rental, from compact machines to large dozers and excavators, with transportation across the UAE and GCC. Rental applies to our used / plant-hire fleet.

Do you supply spare parts?

We hold a large inventory of genuine, OEM and specialised spare parts to keep your equipment running with minimal downtime. Tell us your machine model and the part you need and we'll source it.

Do you offer servicing and after-sales support?

Yes. We provide maintenance, repairs, and technical after-sales support to keep your fleet productive throughout its life.

Which countries do you serve? Do you export?

We have served customers in 30+ countries, including across the GCC, wider MENA, Africa, the CIS and beyond. We can arrange export and logistics, share your destination and requirements when you enquire.

How do I request a quote?

Click any "Inquire" or "Request a Quote" button, or use the enquiry form on our homepage. Tell us the model(s), quantity, timeline and your location, and our team will respond, usually within one business day. You can also call 04 328 7211 or WhatsApp 050 646 9005.

Do you arrange delivery and transportation?

Yes. We operate our own low-bed and flatbed trailers for heavy haulage across the UAE and GCC, and can arrange delivery to your site.

Is there a warranty on equipment and parts?

New equipment and genuine parts carry the applicable manufacturer's warranty. Used equipment is sold as-is unless a specific warranty is agreed in writing. Details are confirmed on your quotation or sales contract.

Where are you located and what are your hours?

Al Wasit Building, Ras Al Khor Industrial Area 2, Dubai, UAE, with branches in Dubai and Jebel Ali. We're open Monday–Friday 9:00–17:00 and Saturday 9:00–14:00.
