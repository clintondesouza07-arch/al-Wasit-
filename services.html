Services & Support

More Than a Machine

For over four decades, Al Wasit has supplied construction equipment across the Middle East, Africa, Asia and the CIS, trusted by contractors in more than 30 countries. Among the first in the region to trade in both new and used machinery, we are a one-stop source for earthmoving and road equipment, materials handling, work tools, attachments and spare parts, backed by our own Plant Hire & Transportation division.

Plant Hire & Transportation

Flexible short and long-term rental, from large dozers to compact welding generators, plus heavy haulage on our own low-bed and flatbed trailers across the UAE and GCC.

Spare Parts

A large inventory of OEM and specialised spare parts for minimal downtime and maximum productivity on site.

Work Tools & Attachments

Buckets, breakers, grapples and attachments from leading global makers and our own AWM-branded line, built for Middle East conditions.

After-Sales Support

Personal, efficient support from first enquiry to after-sales, we deliver solutions, not just equipment.
